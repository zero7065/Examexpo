1. Why this matters now

Nigerian students are sitting the same high-stakes exams with the same overcrowded classrooms, the same thin materials, and the same one-tutor-to-hundreds ratio. In the 2025 UTME, about 75% of candidates scored below 200 out of 400. The pattern is not a surprise to any principal who has watched a strong student freeze in a CBT hall. What has changed is that the exam itself is going digital: WAEC and NECO have directed full CBT, and JAMB has floated a personal-computer option from 2027. A school that prepares students for the paper era is preparing them for an exam that no longer exists.

Preparation is the part that can still be fixed in one term, and that is the part we are offering to carry with you.

2. Our proposal

ExamPadi AI partners with your school to put the platform in front of every student. The school pays nothing to begin, earns a share of every subscription that comes through its own code, and gets a dedicated technical partner for the whole session.

3. What ExamPadi AI delivers

Pattern-based question bank

Past questions across JAMB, WAEC, NECO, NABTEB, and POST-UTME, arranged by topic and exam style so students drill the pattern that actually repeats.

CBT simulator that feels real

Timed, scored, and laid out like the hall. Students learn the interface, the clock, and the nerve before the day itself.

Built for limited data

Light pages, offline practice, and a build that holds up on a shared phone and a weak signal. No school needs to fund a lab to start.

24/7 AI tutor

Students photograph any question or upload a document; the tutor walks them through it step by step, in plain language, at their level.

Teacher dashboard

Class performance, weak-topic heatmaps, assignments, and announcements in one place, so support is aimed where it is needed.

Your material, your control

Teachers can set and share their own material. Nothing leaves the school's control, and nothing is used to train anyone else's model.

9. Rollout plan

  • Week 1 — Onboarding. Teacher accounts created, classes set up, one class selected for the pilot.
  • Week 2–3 — Pilot. One class uses ExamPadi daily; teachers review the dashboard weekly; we adjust settings on their feedback.

Pilot success metrics: minimum 20 minutes/day of practice per student; target +15% accuracy improvement from week 1 to week 2; target 5+ hours/week of teacher time saved on question prep; 80%+ student satisfaction.

  • Week 4 — Wider rollout. Extend to every interested class, with a second training pass for any new teachers.
  • Ongoing — Support. Monthly usage and performance reviews, plus continuous platform improvements based on teacher feedback.

10. Refund policy and responsible use

Yearly Pro includes a 14-day money-back guarantee if the student has used fewer than 30 AI messages. Monthly Pro is non-refundable after 48 hours except in cases of technical failure. AI top-up packs are non-refundable once purchased.

Student data belongs to the student and the school. We do not sell it, we do not use it to train third-party models, and teachers can export or delete class records on request. We comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation. AI output is a study aid, not a marking authority — teachers remain the final judge of a student's work.
